# Cars

The low-poly cars blender/scripts/build_cars.py makes the garage's cars from, by
[Quaternius](https://quaternius.com/packs/cars.html) (the [Cars Bundle on Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza/bundle/Cars-Bundle-FE5IWe6OMk)),
released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain): free to
use, change and ship, no credit required (given here anyway).

- `sports-car.glb`: the saloon the BMW is made from (its "Sports Car", a three-box saloon).
- `sports-car-2.glb`: the coupé the Lambos and the Ferraris are made from (its other "Sports Car").
- `police.glb`, `taxi.glb`, `suv.glb`, `car.glb`, `hatch.glb`: the traffic that drives round by itself (blender/scripts/build_traffic.py).
